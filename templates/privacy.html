Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Dynamic Cells Helper Chrome extension operates.

1. Data Collection

The Dynamic Cells Helper extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension does not read, access, or process any information from the pages you visit.

2. What the Extension Does

The extension adds a single button to the e-prescription.gr website. When clicked, this button opens the Dynamic Cells medical prescription application (app.dynamiccells.com) in a new window. No data is exchanged between the two platforms.

3. Permissions

The extension only requests access to the e-prescription.gr domain, solely for the purpose of displaying the button. No other permissions are requested or used.

4. Third Party Services

The extension links to app.dynamiccells.com. Please refer to the Dynamic Cells application privacy policy for information about data handled within that application.
